Introduction to Blockchain Technology

1. Overview

Blockchain is a distributed digital ledger that records transactions across a network of computers.

2. How Blockchain Works

1. A user initiates a transaction.
2. The transaction is broadcast to the blockchain network.
3. Network participants validate the transaction.
4. Validated transactions are added to a block.
5. The block is added to the blockchain.

3. Key Features

Decentralisation

Blockchain networks can operate without a single central authority controlling the ledger.

Transparency

Public blockchain transactions can be viewed using blockchain explorers.

Security

Cryptographic techniques help protect blockchain records and transactions.

Immutability

Confirmed blockchain records are designed to resist unauthorised changes.

4. What Are Smart Contracts?

Smart contracts are programs stored and executed on blockchain networks. They automatically perform predefined actions when specified conditions are met.

5. Examples of Blockchain Applications

- Cryptocurrency payments.
- Decentralised finance (DeFi).
- Digital identity.
- Supply chain tracking.
- Digital asset ownership.

6. Conclusion

Blockchain technology provides a way to record and verify digital transactions across distributed networks. Understanding its basic concepts is an important foundation for learning Web3.
