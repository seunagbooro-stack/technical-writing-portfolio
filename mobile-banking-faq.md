Mobile Banking App – Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I create an account?

Download the mobile banking app from the official app store. Open it and follow the registration instructions.

2. What should I do if I forget my password?

Select Forgot Password on the login screen. Follow the instructions to reset your password using your registered email address or phone number.

3. Why is my banking app not opening?

The app may not open because of an unstable internet connection or an outdated version.

Solution: Check your internet connection and update the app through the official app store.

4. How do I update the app?

Open the Google Play Store, search for the banking app and select Update if an update is available.

5. What should I do if a transaction fails?

Check your transaction history before trying again. If money was deducted, contact your bank through its official customer support channels.

6. How do I contact customer support?

Open the app and look for its Help, Support or Contact Us section. Use the official contact details provided there.

Security Reminder

Never share your password, PIN or one-time password (OTP) with anyone, including someone claiming to be a bank employee.
